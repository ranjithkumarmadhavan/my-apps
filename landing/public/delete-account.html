Delete your account

For SplitEasy and every other app on apps.ranjithkumarmadhavan.com (they share one account).

How to delete it

  1. Open SplitEasy (website or Android app) and sign in.
  2. Tap your initials at the top right to open Your account.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password if you have one, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address, password, Google sign-in link, UPI ID and settings, permanently.
  • Groups you created that nobody else with an account belongs to, with all their expenses.

What is kept

  • In groups shared with other people, your name stays as a name-only guest, with the expenses and payments involving you. This keeps everyone else's balances correct. It is no longer linked to any account.
  • Groups you created that others still use are handed to another member who has an account.

Can't sign in?

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen first. If that doesn't work, contact Ranjith through ranjithkumarmadhavan.com with the email address of your account, and it will be deleted for you.

See also the privacy policy.
