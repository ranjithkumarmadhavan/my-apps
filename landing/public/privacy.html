Privacy Policy

Applies to apps.ranjithkumarmadhavan.com and every app on it, including SplitEasy. Last updated 18 October 2026.

These are small personal apps built by Ranjith Kumar Madhavan. They collect only what is needed to work, and never sell your data or show ads.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) if you set one.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter in the apps: for SplitEasy, that means groups, the names of people you add, expenses, payments, and an optional UPI ID so friends can pay you.
  • Security data: the IP address of failed sign-in attempts, kept for 15 minutes to block password guessing.

How it is used

Only to run the apps: signing you in, showing your groups and balances, and emailing password-reset links you ask for. People in a SplitEasy group can see that group's members, expenses, payments and UPI IDs.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Payments

SplitEasy never processes or holds money. “Pay” buttons only open your own UPI app (such as GPay, PhonePe or CRED) with the details filled in; the payment happens entirely in that app.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is stored on our hosting provider's servers (Hostinger) and, if you use Google sign-in, Google confirms your identity.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from the account page in SplitEasy; here is how, and exactly what is deleted. In groups shared with other people, your name stays as a name-only guest with its expenses, so everyone else's balances stay correct. To get a copy of your data, contact Ranjith through ranjithkumarmadhavan.com.

Data kept on your device

So the app opens instantly and stays readable offline, a copy of the groups you have opened is stored in your browser on that device. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
